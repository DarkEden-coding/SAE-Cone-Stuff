Keypoint geometry is measured from the BRT training labels (median over ~8k boxes ≥90 px tall), as heights and half-widths relative to box height, then scaled to FS cone heights. Small cones (0.325 m): pairs 0/1, 2/3, 4/5 at 0.228, 0.150, 0.064 m with half-widths 0.040, 0.054, 0.075 m — stripe top, stripe bottom, body foot. Big orange cones (0.505 m) use all eight points: pairs 0/1…6/7 at 0.380, 0.300, 0.220, 0.140 m with half-widths 0.042, 0.053, 0.060, 0.070 m — the edges of the two white stripes. The solved origin is the cone's ground point.

Each cone is solved with OpenCV planar PnP (IPPE), keeping the positive-depth candidate with lowest reprojection error. A solve needs ≥4 confident points, box height ≥12 px, depth <80 m and RMS ≤ max(3 px, 10% of box height).

The viewer fits a ground plane through the solved base centres (least squares, tilt-limited) and re-expresses everything in that frame, so the camera's pitch is removed and cones stand upright. The per-cone PnP rotation is not used: a camera-facing planar proxy makes it unreliable. Distances depend on the assumed intrinsics and cone sizes; use measured calibration for real localization.
